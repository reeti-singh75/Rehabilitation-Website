What to do right now?

1

Pause

Promise yourself to wait just 24 hours. Don't act on these feelings today.

2

Safety

Move away from anything dangerous. Go to a room with people or a public place.

3

Breathe

Focus only on your breath. Inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 7, exhale for 8.

4

Talk

Call one of the numbers above. Just say "I'm not okay, I need to talk."
